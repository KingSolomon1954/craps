Detailed Description

A Window physically contained within a full screen Surface.

  • Concrete Panel classes are owned their full screen window
  • Panels do not participate with SurfaceManager pushing or popping
  • Panels do not draw borders
  • Panels are placed inside borders already drawn by their full screen owner
  • SurfaceBase contains the ncurses pWin_ WINDOW*
  • Panels do register with SurfaceManager to participate in clean shutdown
