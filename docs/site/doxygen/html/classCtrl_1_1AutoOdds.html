Detailed Description

One player's automatic odds settings.

Holds, per bet type (PassLine, DontPass, Come, DontCome) and point (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10), what to put on as odds: either a number of times the contract amount or an exact dollar amount. Nothing set means no automatic odds for that spot. A master switch turns the whole thing on and off without losing the settings.

plan() is the pass: it looks at the bets with a point and no odds that it has not handled yet and says what odds to place. Whatever happens to those odds, by the table or by the player, the bet is handled and is not tried again, so odds the player takes down stay down. The handled bets are not saved; they are forgotten on load and with forgetHandled(), and a bet that is gone is dropped from the list.

Persistence, as the "AutoOdds" key of the player's Prefs:

AutoOdds:
enabled: true
multiples:
PassLine: {4: 3x, 5: 4x, 6: 5x, 8: 5x, 9: 4x, 10: 3x}
Come: {6: $50}
