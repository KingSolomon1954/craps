Detailed Description

Undo of bet changes, a stack for each player.

Supported (stack-based undo, limited to bets)

  • Scope: only bet state (no configuration, no gameplay state, no rolls).
  • Per player: each player has their own stack, so undo only ever touches the bets of the player who asked. The View asks for the user player.
  • Granularity: one bet change = one undo step. A batch can be made one step with push(..., joinPrevious=true).
  • Kinds of changes:
  • new bet placed
  • existing bet changed (contract or odds amount)
  • bet removed
  • Ordering: last-in, first-out (classic undo stack).
  • Lifetime: cleared when the dice are rolled, so only decisions made since the last roll can be undone, and when the table changes. A player whose session ends loses their stack too.
  • Betting must be open. While the dice are rolling there is no undo, and a refused undo keeps its step.
  • A failed undo drops that player's history (see undo()).

Not supported (explicit exclusions)

  • Undoing out of order changes (no random-access undo).
  • Redo.
  • Anything outside of bets (like table config, game options).

Records a change the player just made. Call after the change succeeded. With joinPrevious the change becomes part of the step on top of the stack instead of a new step, so a batch such as taking down every removable bet is one undo.

◆ undo()

Gen::ReturnCode UndoManager::undo ( const Craps::PlayerId & playerId,
std::string & description,
Gen::ErrorPass & ep )

Undoes the player's most recent step. On success description says what was done. On failure ep has the reason, and since the player's bets may no longer match what was recorded, that player's whole history is dropped. Nothing is undone if there is nothing to undo.
